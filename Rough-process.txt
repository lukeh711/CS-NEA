~~EQUATIONS~~
(assuming a, r, V and F are vector2s)

1. F = [G]m1m2(r2-r1)/∥r2-r1∥^3

In: G(const), mass1, mass2, pos1, pos2
Out: resultant force

2. a=F/m

In: resultant force, mass1
Out: acceleration


3. Leapfrog DKD
-D1/2: rmid​=r+v⋅(2Δt​)
-K: {vnew​=v+a(rmid​)⋅Δt}
-D1/2: rnew​=rmid​+vnew​⋅(2Δt​)

In: Position, velocity, acceleration
Out: new position, new velocity
